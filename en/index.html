No install needed. Read every word: each one has a trap.

Here are a few (turn on JavaScript to play for real).

  1. Team A scored the first goal and the last goal of the match. Did Team A win?

    Answer

    Not necessarily. Not necessarily. A leads 1–0, B scores 3, then A scores again: A got the first and last goals but lost 2–3.

  2. 10 candles are burning. You blow out 3 of them. How many candles are still burning?

    Answer

    7. 7. The famous riddle asks what's left the next morning; this one asks about right now, so 10 − 3 = 7 still burn.

  3. It's not true that Lan didn't eat the last spring roll. Who ate the last spring roll?

    Answer

    Lan. Lan. 'Not true that she didn't' cancels out to 'she did'.

  4. If a week runs from Monday to Sunday, which day is right in the middle of it?

    Answer

    Thursday. 7 days, so the middle is day 4: Mon, Tue, Wed, then Thursday, then Fri, Sat, Sun.

  5. On the Moon, an astronaut lets go of a sturdy helium balloon. It…

    Answer

    Falls to the ground. It falls. A balloon only rises by being lighter than the air around it, and the Moon has no air.

FAQ

Is Ngu Meter free?

Completely free. No ads and no in-app purchases.

Is it an IQ test?

No. The chaos score is just for fun and doesn't measure anyone's intelligence or worth. The jokes are about the situations, never about you.

Do I need the internet?

No. Questions, the daily quiz and challenges all work offline. A challenge code carries everything needed to rebuild the exact round.

How do challenges work?

After a round, share the result image or the 8-character code. Friends scan the QR or tap “Got a challenge code?” and paste it to play your exact 10 questions.

Does the app collect data?

No. No account, no tracking, no server. History and achievements live only on your device.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes. It supports iPhone and iPad, in any orientation on iPad.

FAQ

Is Ngu Meter free?

Completely free. No ads and no in-app purchases.

Is it an IQ test?

No. The chaos score is just for fun and doesn't measure anyone's intelligence or worth. The jokes are about the situations, never about you.

Do I need the internet?

No. Questions, the daily quiz and challenges all work offline. A challenge code carries everything needed to rebuild the exact round.

How do challenges work?

After a round, share the result image or the 8-character code. Friends scan the QR or tap “Got a challenge code?” and paste it to play your exact 10 questions.

Does the app collect data?

No. No account, no tracking, no server. History and achievements live only on your device.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes. It supports iPhone and iPad, in any orientation on iPad.
